# ergPLAN 1.0 — release note

1 November 2026 · jouleWise | ergPLAN · build tag `stage-8-done`

ergPLAN works out what green power would cost a client compared with the grid, how much of it to buy or build, and what the DISCOM's rules do to that number. Every rate on a client screen cites the regulation, the page and the quote, and opens the stored PDF at that page.

## What a client can do

1. **Drop their electricity bills.** billx reads them; anything billx could not verify is asked back, one value at a time. Or they can type, speak or chat the site's facts instead.
2. **See today's cost rebuilt** from the approved tariff, line by line beside the real bill, with the differences explained by named gaps.
3. **Compare every green source against the grid** per time-of-day slot: open-access solar, wind and hybrid (intra-state and ISTS), rooftop, batteries, IEX segments, group captive. Each source has a cited cost waterfall.
4. **See the load profile** synthesised from twelve bills (monthly energy exact, ToD split and maximum demand matched), or upload meter data.
5. **Get three sized options** (captive solar; hybrid with battery; rooftop, solar and battery), solved at 15 minutes for the year. Every option is settled again under the state's banking rules before anyone sees it.
6. **Share a live plan link.** It shows the recommended option, the options compared, the binding constraints, what-if questions by chip, voice or text, a PDF with numbered citations, and the documents appendix. jouleWise analysts see the full working behind every number.

## Where it is sourced

| State | DISCOMs | Approved rules |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Uttar Pradesh | NPCL, PVVNL, MVVNL, PuVVNL, DVVNL, KESCO | Tariff, ToD, open-access charges and losses, banking and settlement |
| Maharashtra | MSEDCL | Tariff, ToD, cross-subsidy surcharge, banking and settlement |
| Karnataka | BESCOM, MESCOM, CESC, HESCOM, GESCOM | Tariff (HT-2(a)), ToD |
| Telangana | TGSPDCL, TGNPDCL | Tariff (HT-I(A), kVAh), ToD |
| Punjab | PSPCL | Tariff (large supply, general industry) |
| Gujarat, Rajasthan, Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh, Haryana | — | Not yet sourced: the screens say so |

There are 138 golden tests across the approved rulebooks. In Karnataka, Telangana and Punjab, open-access charges and banking are not yet sourced, so open-access options there show "not yet sourced" rather than a number.

## Under the hood

- Rulebooks are versioned, audited by trigger, and approved by an analyst only when every golden test passes. Engines read approved rules only.
- Bill reading is billx only. The regulation reader proposes draft rules with verbatim quotes that must be found on the page. No model ever decides a number.
- Runs pin every rule version, the price vintage, the load profile and the solver, and replay to the paisa.
- Sign-in is by email link, with analyst and client roles. Clients see only their own sites. Share links work by token.
