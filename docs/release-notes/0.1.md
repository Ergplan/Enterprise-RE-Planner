# billx 0.1: the embedded bill engine

14 Oct 2026. The first image esgOS can run: contract 1.0 exactly, contract 1.1 on top, in one
container. Built from build prompt 5.0, Stages 1 and 2.

## What it is

A headless HTTP service that reads Indian electricity bills (and gas bills generically) into
exact values, verifies each value by two independent readings, checks the bill's own
arithmetic, recomputes every charge from its printed quantity and rate, and returns the
result under esgOS's bill engine contract. It is **a bill-reading service with basic
self-checks, not a regulatory bill auditor**: every bill says `audit_status: not_audited`
until the tariff engine exists (build prompt 4.2 Stage 5b).

## Contracts served

- `BILLX_API_MODE=esgos-1.0`: contract 1.0 word for word (`contract/esgos-1.0/contract.md`).
- `BILLX_API_MODE=esgos-1.1` (default): contract 1.1 (`contract/esgos-1.1/contract.md`;
  what it adds and why: `docs/contract-1.1.md`). esgOS consumes `accepted`.
- `contract/openapi.json` is generated from the app; `contract/examples/<mode>/` holds
  nine synthetic examples per mode (one per route and per failure), which `tests/unit/
  test_contract_examples.py` runs against the app.

## Image

`registry.gitlab.com/icac1/ocr-bill-extraction/billx:0.1`, for `linux/amd64` and
`linux/arm64`; port 8080; non-root; the root filesystem may be read-only with a tmpfs on
`/tmp`; writes only under `/data`. Local OCR is built in: PaddleOCR (models in the image) on
arm64, RapidOCR on amd64. No network is needed at run time except, when a job allows it, the
model providers.

| Platform | Size | Memory, peak (four bills submitted at once) | OCR engine |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| linux/arm64 | 570 MB | 1850 MiB with the default two workers; 2061 MiB with four | PaddleOCR |
| linux/amd64 | 709 MB (built under emulation on the Mac) | not measured here (emulation); CI's native runner measures it with `tools/image/measure_memory.sh` | RapidOCR (PaddleOCR fails inside oneDNN; the engine probe falls back at start) |

The image runs **two jobs at once by default** (`BILLX_MAX_JOBS=2`): with OCR serialised
inside one container, two workers finish four bills in the same 100 s as four workers do,
within 2 GB. Four jobs are still accepted at once and queued. One digital bill takes about
26 s end to end on this Mac's arm64 Docker.

Settings: `BILLX_TOKEN` (required), `BILLX_API_MODE`, `BILLX_DATA_DIR=/data`,
`BILLX_MAX_JOBS` (2 in the image), `BILLX_QUEUE_LIMIT` (32), `BILLX_RETENTION_HOURS` (24),
`BILLX_OPENAI_API_KEY`, `BILLX_ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`. `docker/compose.yaml` shows the wiring
(`BILL_ENGINE_URL=http://bill-engine:8080`, `BILL_ENGINE_TOKEN`).

## Limits

25 MB and 30 pages per file; four jobs accepted at once (two run, the rest queue), 429 with
`Retry-After` beyond the queue limit; files and results deleted within 24 hours. About 12 to 25 s per page of local
OCR on a CPU; a scan or photo also needs one or two model calls (the caller's
`allow_cloud_model` and `max_cost_usd`).

## What `profile.status` and `audit_status` mean

- `profile.status`: `final` (a layout that met its launch gate: none yet), `draft` (a profile
  exists but is unmeasured: AVVNL and PVVNL, drafted from synthetic layouts), `none` (no
  profile: read by the generic ladder). A downstream report labels figures from a `draft` or
  `none` layout as from an unverified layout.
- `audit_status`: `not_audited` on every bill in 0.1.

## What is unmeasured

No real bill has been confirmed yet. Accuracy on real bills is `unmeasured`; the engine's
own `proof` and `readers` on every value (1.1) say how each was read. On the synthetic set:
every digital PDF passes with no model call, every scan passes with two model families, and
all 48 planted misreads are caught by the check that declares them.

## Known gaps

Anchors, photo preparation and the quality gate (4.2 Stage 4): a phone photo may end
`needs_review` where its OCR and the model disagree. Password-protected PDFs end `unreadable`
with `password_required`. The tariff-order recheck, insights, hosting, permanent storage and
the web app are deferred (build prompt 5.0 section 14).
