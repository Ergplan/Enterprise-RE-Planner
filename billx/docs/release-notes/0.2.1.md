# billx 0.2.1: net payable after the due-date rebate

27 Oct 2026. One definition from the team's review of 0.2 (pending item 20).

- `totals.net_payable` is the amount payable by the due date, **after** any due-date
  (prompt payment) rebate, on every bill (contract 1.0 and 1.1). On a bill without such a
  rebate nothing changes. Never the amount payable after the due date.
- New optional field `totals.due_date_rebate` (contract 1.1): the rebate the net payable
  deducts, as a positive amount.
- Check G09: current charges + arrears + adjustments - due-date rebate = net payable, within ₹1.
- The PVVNL profile hint names 'Payable by Due date' (monthly bills) and 'Amount Payable
  before Due Date (Rebate@1%)' (open-access adjustment bills) as the net payable, and
  'Due Date Rebate (₹)' as the rebate.

ENGINE_VERSION 0.2.1. The studio re-reads a bill with this engine when it is opened.

## For the team

Six PVVNL monthly bills had been confirmed with the amount before the rebate, as 0.2's hint
said. The team confirmed the rule on 27 Oct, and their net payable was corrected to the
printed 'Payable by Due date' (each equal to 'Payable Amount' - 'Due Date Rebate (₹)' within
₹1), with the rebate recorded, as manifest corrections. They were the bills of the one
layout known to print a due-date rebate whose confirmed net payable equalled current
charges + arrears; the other layouts' confirmations were not rechecked bill by bill.
