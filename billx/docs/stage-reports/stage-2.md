## What changed

- **API (2.1):** `billx/platform/api/app.py`: the five contract calls and nothing else under
  `/v1`, bearer auth in constant time, the error table from one handler, `X-Request-Id`
  echoed and the `traceparent` trace id on every log line, `kind: "text"`, the 1.1 options
  (`wait`, `contact`, `account_ref`, `pdf_password`), 400 for a 1.1 option in 1.0 mode;
  `billx/platform/api/render.py`: contract 1.0's exact wire shape and 1.1's additions from a
  `BillResult`; `billx serve` with JSON logs.
- **Store (2.2):** `billx/platform/store/`: the SQLite job store (WAL, idempotent
  `request_id`, 409 on a different file, jobs survive a restart), a folder per job, the
  bounded in-process queue (at least 4 workers, 429 with `Retry-After`), the 24-hour sweeper
  every 10 minutes.
- **Health, metrics, logs (2.3):** `GET /v1/health` with `ocr` and `cloud_model`;
  `/metrics` with counts, job seconds, queue depth; the no-values-in-logs test runs a bill
  with a distinctive value through the API and searches every log line, the metrics and an
  error body for it.
- **Contracts (2.4):** `contract/esgos-1.1/contract.md`, `docs/contract-1.1.md`, `docs/api.md`,
  `contract/openapi.json` generated by `billx openapi` and checked by CI, `contract/examples/`
  written by `billx examples` from the synthetic set of seed 1.
- **Image and CI (2.5):** `docker/engine.Dockerfile` (both OCR runtimes installed, PaddleOCR's
  models downloaded at build time by a warm-up, non-root, `/data` the only writable path,
  health check without curl), `docker/compose.yaml` (read-only root, tmpfs `/tmp`, 2 GB
  limit, the esgOS wiring), `.gitlab-ci.yml` (checks, gitleaks, multi-platform images to the
  GitLab registry, the identifier search over the amd64 image, the release copy to esgOS's
  registry on a tag when its variables exist, the native amd64 OCR check),
  `tools/image/measure_memory.sh`.
- **Release 0.1 (2.6):** `docs/release-notes/0.1.md`; tag `0.1` on `main`.

## Image figures

- linux/arm64 (built on the Mac through Docker Desktop's proxy): 570 MB; build steps apt
  about 20 s, pip 120 to 170 s, OCR warm-up 70 to 110 s, export 21 s.
- Memory, four digital bills submitted at once: 2061 MiB peak with four workers, 1850 MiB
  with two; both finish in about 100 s (OCR serialised). The image ships with
  `BILLX_MAX_JOBS=2`.
- linux/amd64, built under emulation on the Mac: 709 MB; pip 559 s and warm-up 81 s under
  QEMU; the no-network read-only warm-up picks RapidOCR (the predict-time probe at engine
  init falls back, as Stage 0 found PaddleOCR failing inside oneDNN on amd64). Its memory is
  for CI's native runner to measure.

## Decisions

`docs/decisions.md` under "Stage 2": a printed inconsistency counts as arithmetic coverage;
1.1 `notes` carry no reasons or flags; the store's defaults (max jobs raised to 4,
`Retry-After` from the median job time, retention from `submitted_at`); the engine reads every
guaranteed field in both modes and 1.0 renders its subset.
